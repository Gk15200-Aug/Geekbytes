Choosing a CRM and marketing automation platform is one of the most consequential tech decisions a business makes. In 2026, the three platforms that dominate small business and agency conversations are **GoHighLevel**, **HubSpot**, and **ActiveCampaign**.

This guide compares all three across pricing, features, ease of use, and ideal use cases — so you can make the right call for your business.

## GoHighLevel: Best for Agencies and Service Businesses

GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one platform built specifically for marketing agencies and service-based businesses. It packs CRM, email marketing, SMS automation, funnel builder, appointment scheduling, reputation management, and even website hosting into one platform.

### Pros
- **True all-in-one**: Replaces ClickFunnels, Calendly, Mailchimp, and more
- **White-label**: Sell GHL as your own branded software to clients
- **Sub-accounts**: Manage multiple clients from one dashboard
- **Unlimited contacts** on all plans
- **Active community** and frequent updates

### Cons
- Steeper learning curve than simpler CRMs
- Not ideal for complex B2B pipeline management
- Some native integrations feel basic compared to dedicated tools

### GoHighLevel Pricing (2026)
- **Starter**: $97/month — 3 sub-accounts, all core features
- **Unlimited**: $297/month — unlimited sub-accounts, API access
- **White Label**: $497/month — fully branded SaaS platform

**Best for**: Marketing agencies, coaches, consultants, local service businesses, real estate agents.

## HubSpot: Best for B2B and Inbound Marketing

HubSpot is the gold standard for inbound marketing, content-driven lead generation, and enterprise CRM. It has a truly free CRM tier and scales up with feature-rich paid hubs.

### Pros
- Best-in-class reporting and analytics
- Excellent content and SEO tools
- Free tier is genuinely useful
- Deep integrations with almost everything

### Cons
- Gets expensive fast — full suite can exceed $3,000/month
- Contacts-based pricing penalises growth
- No white-label option
- SMS is limited and expensive vs GHL

### HubSpot Pricing (2026)
- **Free CRM**: $0 — basic contacts and deals
- **Starter**: $20/month per seat
- **Professional**: $890/month (includes automation, A/B testing)
- **Enterprise**: $3,600/month

**Best for**: B2B companies, SaaS startups, content-driven businesses, sales-heavy teams.

## ActiveCampaign: Best for Email Automation

ActiveCampaign has long been the favourite of email marketers who want powerful automation without HubSpot's price tag. Its email automation builder is arguably the best in the market.

### Pros
- World-class email automation with visual builder
- Strong eCommerce integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce)
- Competitive pricing for small lists
- Built-in CRM included

### Cons
- No built-in funnel builder
- No white-label option
- Phone/SMS requires integration with Twilio
- Less suited for agencies managing multiple clients

### ActiveCampaign Pricing (2026)
- **Starter**: $15/month (1,000 contacts)
- **Plus**: $49/month
- **Professional**: $79/month
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing

**Best for**: eCommerce businesses, email-first marketers, solopreneurs, and newsletter businesses.

## Which CRM Should You Choose?

**Choose GoHighLevel if**:
- You're a marketing agency managing multiple clients
- You want an all-in-one platform (funnels + CRM + SMS + booking)
- You want to white-label and resell the software
- You need unlimited contacts without paying per contact

**Choose HubSpot if**:
- You're a B2B company doing inbound content marketing
- You need best-in-class reporting and analytics
- Your team already uses HubSpot integrations (Salesforce, etc.)
- You're funded and can absorb the premium pricing

**Choose ActiveCampaign if**:
- Email automation is your primary focus
- You run an eCommerce store on Shopify or WooCommerce
- You have a relatively small list but complex automation needs
