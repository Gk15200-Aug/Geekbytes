## The 7 Core Features You Need to Understand

### 1. CRM & Pipeline Management

GHL's CRM lets you track every lead and client through a visual kanban pipeline. Unlike HubSpot (where basic features are free but key features cost thousands), GHL includes full pipeline management in every plan.

You can have multiple pipelines — one for prospects, one for onboarding, one for client projects, one for upsells. Each pipeline has stages you define, and leads move through automatically based on triggers.

### 2. Two-Way SMS & Email

GHL gives you a single inbox for all SMS and email communication with contacts. This is genuinely useful — you can see the entire conversation history across channels in one place.

Two-way SMS is particularly powerful. Response rates for SMS follow-ups average 45% — compared to 6% for email. Setting up a simple 3-touch SMS sequence for new leads can double your contact rate overnight.

### 3. Automation Workflows

This is where GHL earns its price tag. Workflows are visual automation builders that trigger on events (new lead, form submission, call missed, deal stage changed) and execute actions (send SMS, send email, create task, add tag, move pipeline, call webhook).

A well-built automation stack in GHL can:
- Follow up with new leads within 60 seconds, 24/7
- Nurture cold leads over a 90-day sequence
- Book appointments without human involvement
- Send review requests after job completion
- Trigger onboarding sequences when a deal closes

### 4. Funnel & Website Builder

GHL includes a full drag-and-drop funnel and website builder. It's not as polished as ClickFunnels for funnel design, but it's good enough for most businesses — and having everything under one roof avoids the integration headaches of separate tools.

### 5. Calendar & Appointment Booking

Natively integrated with the CRM. Prospects self-book from your calendar, and the appointment triggers automations — confirmation SMS, reminder sequences, no-show follow-ups.

### 6. Reputation Management

Automated review request sequences via SMS and email, with a dashboard monitoring your Google and Facebook ratings. For local businesses, this alone often justifies the GHL subscription.

### 7. White-Label SaaS (Agency Plan)

On the Agency Unlimited plan, you can white-label GHL entirely under your brand. Your clients log in to "YourAgencyApp.com" powered by GHL — they never see the GoHighLevel brand. You can sell this as a software product, charging clients monthly for access.

## The 5 GHL Automations That Pay for Themselves in Week 1

### 1. The 5-Minute Lead Response

Trigger: New lead created (from any source)
Actions: Send immediate SMS ("Hi [Name], thanks for your enquiry. I'll call you within 15 minutes.") → Create a call task for your sales team → If no task completed in 4 hours, send follow-up SMS

**Why it works:** Responding within 5 minutes makes you 21× more likely to qualify the lead. Most businesses take 48+ hours. This automation alone can double your contact rate.

### 2. The 30-Day Ghost Lead Resurrector

Trigger: Lead hasn't responded in 7 days
Actions: SMS drip sequence over 30 days with varied messaging — educational content, case studies, offers, urgency

**Why it works:** 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups. Most salespeople give up after 2. Automation removes the human inconsistency.

### 3. The Review Request Sequence

Trigger: Deal marked as "Won" or project completed
Actions: Wait 24 hours → SMS asking for a Google review (with direct link) → If no review after 7 days, email follow-up → If no review after 14 days, final SMS

**Why it works:** Most happy customers never leave a review because no one asks them at the right moment. This sequence consistently generates 3–8× more reviews.

### 4. Appointment No-Show Recovery

Trigger: Appointment status changed to "No-Show"
Actions: SMS within 5 minutes ("Missed you! Let's reschedule — pick a time here [link]") → Email follow-up → If no rebook in 48 hours, sales team task

**Why it works:** No-shows aren't always disinterested — often they just forgot or had a conflict. Re-engaging within minutes of the no-show catches them while they still feel guilty about it.

### 5. The Post-Onboarding Upsell

Trigger: Client reaches "Onboarding Complete" stage in pipeline
Actions: Wait 30 days → Email highlighting additional services → SMS offering a free strategy call → Create follow-up task for account manager

**Why it works:** Selling to existing clients is 7× cheaper than acquiring new ones. Most businesses are terrible at structured upselling — automation makes it systematic.

## Common GoHighLevel Setup Mistakes

**Skipping the naming conventions.** With 50+ automations and 20+ pipelines across multiple client sub-accounts, poor naming creates chaos. Establish a naming convention before you build anything: `[Client]-[Trigger]-[Action]-v1`.

**Building automations before testing lead flow.** Get a lead through your funnel manually first. Understand every touchpoint before automating them.

**Not setting up DMARC/DKIM for email.** Email deliverability is critical. If you skip the DNS configuration for your sending domain, your GHL emails will land in spam. Google's 2024 requirements made this non-negotiable.

**Using GHL's website builder for complex sites.** GHL's website builder works for simple landing pages and funnels. For your main company website or a client's primary web presence, use a proper CMS or web development stack.
